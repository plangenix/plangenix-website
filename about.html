About

Hi, I'm Mandip

I'm a construction planner based in Sydney, with over 12 years in Australian construction and infrastructure. I started PlanGenix to work directly with builders on their tender and construction programs.

Background

My Background

I've built programs and run project controls on government, infrastructure, defence, health and commercial work all over Australia.

Along the way I've been a Project Control Analyst at Sydney Trains, a Project Planner at Thales on the Navy's DDG fleet, and an Estimator and Planner at Aurify Constructions, where I ran tenders and managed construction programs from start to finish.

For the past few years I've also been working as a freelance planner with Plannery, one of Australia's most active tender programming firms, preparing programs for contractors across NSW, VIC, QLD, WA and the ACT. Education, health, aged care, community, government and commercial have all been part of the mix.

These days I work directly with builders, developers and clients who need a program done properly and on time.

How I Work

What You Can Expect

The same things on every job, big or small.

🎯

Logic That Holds Up

I check every link, constraint and duration. If someone pulls the program apart, it should still make sense.

⚡

On Time

Tender deadlines are fixed, so I plan my week around yours. If I can't make your date, I'll tell you up front.

🤝

Part of Your Team

The best programs come from talking to the people who will build the job, so I work with your estimator, PM and site team, not around them.

🛡️

Straight Advice

If a date looks unrealistic or the logic doesn't work, I'll say so. Better to hear it from me than from the evaluator.
