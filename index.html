How I Work

How a Job Runs

01

Brief

You send the tender documents and your methodology. I go through the scope, contract and key dates, and ask any questions early.

02

Build the Program

I build the program in P6 or MS Project, following the way you plan to deliver the job.

03

Review

You review the draft. I make the changes and we agree the final version.

04

Deliver

You get the native file and a PDF ready to submit. If the client comes back with questions, I'm still around to help.
