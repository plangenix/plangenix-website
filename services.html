Tender Programming

The tender program shows the client how you will build the job and whether your dates are realistic. Evaluators notice when it doesn't match the methodology, or when the logic doesn't hold up.

I build tender programs that follow your methodology and your commercial strategy, without overdoing it. At tender stage a program doesn't need resource loading. It needs to be clear, realistic and easy for the evaluator to follow.

Construction Programming

Once you've won the job, the baseline program is what your time performance is measured against. It has to match the contract, have sound logic, and be useful to the site team, not just something to hand the Superintendent.

I build construction programs that support contract administration and can be updated easily through the job, from a Level 2 phase program down to a detailed Level 3 or 4 works program.

Contract baseline program (Level 2, 3 or 4)
Logic-driven activity sequences
Key date and milestone tracking
3-week lookahead programs
Program revisions and update management

Typical Program Levels

Level 2: Phase Program
Major work packages and key dependencies
Level 3: Works Program (Most common)
Full activity-level schedule with logic and durations
Level 4: Detailed Works Program
Granular sequencing for complex or high-risk scopes

Project Controls

Without controls you're running the job on gut feel, and by the time a problem shows up it's often too late to recover. I set up controls that give you an early warning while there's still time to act.

How far it goes depends on the job, the contract and what you have to report. It can be simple fortnightly updates or full earned value tracking of cost and time.

Schedule update and maintenance
Earned Value Management (EVM)
Cost and schedule performance indices (CPI / SPI)
Estimate at Completion (EAC) forecasting
Risk register integration and schedule risk analysis

Reporting & Dashboards

Reports should tell your team and your client where the job is at, without anyone having to dig through a Gantt chart. I build everything from weekly lookaheads to Power BI dashboards.

A one-page progress report for a small job or a live dashboard for a big program: either way, I'll set it up so people actually use it.

Weekly 3-week lookahead reports
Monthly project control reports
S-curve and progress tracking charts
Power BI interactive project dashboards
Milestone and key date trackers
